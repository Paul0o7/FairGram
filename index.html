How to get your Instagram data
  1. Open Instagram and go to your profile, then the menu (☰)
  2. Tap Settings and privacy → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions
  3. Tap Download your information, then Some of your information
  4. Select only Followers and following
  5. Set format to JSON and date range to All time, then submit
  6. Instagram will notify you when it's ready (minutes to a couple of days) — download the ZIP and unzip it
  7. Find followers_1.json and following.json inside connections/followers_and_following/
